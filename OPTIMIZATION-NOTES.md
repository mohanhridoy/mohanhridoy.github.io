# Portfolio optimization notes

## Conversion paths

- Primary action: book a 30-minute meeting through Calendly.
- Secondary actions: review selected work, visit LinkedIn, send an email, or download the résumé.
- CTA interactions emit a `portfolio_cta` event to `window.dataLayer` and a `portfolio:cta` browser event. A future analytics tool can consume either event without changing the markup.

## Proof and claims

- 200+ partnerships, 20+ YouTube creators, and 400K+ active installs are presented as career and portfolio context.
- The selected-work cards describe responsibilities and working methods. They do not claim that one activity caused the full portfolio result.
- No testimonial quotation was invented. The credibility section directs visitors to LinkedIn for public recommendations and professional connections.

## Suggested next experiments

1. Compare the current benefit-led hero with a role-led headline such as “Product marketing for WordPress and SaaS.”
2. Compare “Book a meeting” with “Discuss your product” as the primary CTA.
3. When approved examples are available, add one detailed case study with a problem, contribution, timeline, and verified result.
4. Add real recommendation quotes only after receiving permission from each named person.
