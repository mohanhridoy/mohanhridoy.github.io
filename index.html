FAQ

Quick answers to things people ask me.

What kind of products do you work with?

Mostly WordPress plugins and SaaS products. That's where I've spent my whole marketing career, so I know how these products are found, compared and bought.

How do you approach link building?

I start with sites that really fit the product and its audience, then reach out with something useful for them too, like a guest post or a content exchange. Real relationships last longer than one-off links.

Do you only do SEO?

No. SEO is a big part, but I also handle partnerships, email campaigns, influencer outreach, content and social media. They work better together.

Can we work together on a project?

Yes. Send me a short note about the product and what you want to achieve, and we can talk through the scope, deliverables and timeline.
