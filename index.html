What kind of products do you work with?+

Mostly WordPress plugins and SaaS products. That's where I've spent my marketing career, so I understand how these products are found, compared, and bought.

How do you approach link building?+

I start with sites that fit the product and its audience, then reach out with something useful for them too, such as a guest post or content collaboration. Real relationships last longer than one-off links.

Do you only do SEO?+

SEO is a big part of my work, alongside partnerships, email campaigns, influencer outreach, content, and social media. They work better together.

Can we work together on a project?+

Yes. Send me a short note about the product and what you want to achieve. We can talk through the scope, deliverables, and timeline.
